---
name: vertical-slice-delivery
description: Plan and deliver one small end-to-end product capability.
---

Define one user outcome, acceptance conditions, invalid cases, and a strict
scope boundary. Trace interaction, contract, authorization, domain behavior,
persistence, audit/observability, and presentation. Maintain a forward plan
and factual progress record. Record discoveries as decisions or follow-up
slices instead of silently expanding the active slice.
